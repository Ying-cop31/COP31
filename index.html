恶劣天气下的骑手守护 Protecting Riders in Extreme Weather

气候变化让高温、暴雨、台风、寒潮更加频繁，长期户外作业的骑手首当其冲。淘宝闪购用数字化工具守护骑手：实时气象预警、极端天气智能调度与高温"熔断"停单、防暑防寒物资驿站、骑手健康科普。 Climate change is making heatwaves, rainstorms, typhoons and cold snaps more frequent, and riders who work outdoors bear the first impact. Taobao Instant Commerce uses digital tools to protect them: real-time weather alerts, smart dispatch with a high-temperature "circuit-breaker" order pause, heat- and cold-relief stations, and rider health education.
